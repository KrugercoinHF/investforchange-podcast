# EP01 Show Notes – What is InvestForChange?

## Episode Summary

Episode 1 introduces the InvestForChange concept: transforming the SASSA Child Support Grant from a monthly cash disbursement into a lifelong investment vehicle for every South African newborn from 2030.

## Key Points Covered

- Current CSG: R580/month, ~12–13 million children, R185,000–R190,000 lifetime value (inflation-adjusted)
- InvestForChange model: 36-month incubation → activation → lifelong dividends
- 30-year projections: R50,600 (3%) | R90,200 (5%) | R158,700 (7%)
- Every child becomes a shareholder in the InvestForChange Sustainable DigiStocks Fund
